Reading a label

  1. The big number is the one limit that aisle is about, like storage for databases. The 5-step scale beside it compares it with the biggest in the aisle.
  2. The fine print lists the exact limits.
  3. The bottom line covers who can claim it, whether you can sell with it, and whether signup needs a credit card. A check means yes, a cross means no, and a dashed icon means nobody knows yet. The card shows crossed out when you can sign up without one.
  4. The date is when someone last checked the vendor's page. No date means nobody has.
